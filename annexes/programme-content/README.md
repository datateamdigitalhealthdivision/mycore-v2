# Programme Content Annex

The legacy MY Core archive contained a large body of programme-specific questionnaire content, including child-health, maternal, risk-assessment, and composition-form artefacts.

## Editorial treatment in v2

MY Core preserves this material and places it behind the core vendor entry path, so that the baseline profiles and terminology remain the entry point for generic integration.

The vendor-facing computable guide publishes the baseline profiles,
terminologies, example payloads, and naming systems needed for generic
integration. Legacy programme questionnaires and questionnaire responses are
preserved in source lineage and in the programme-content register instead of
being published as first-class conformance artefacts in the maintained FSH
authoring stream under `ig/input/fsh/`.

## Current programme groupings

- child-health questionnaires and sample responses
- maternal questionnaires and sample responses
- risk-assessment instruments
- composition-style forms used as structured programme content

## Preservation model

The preserved inventory is recorded in `mappings/programme-content-register.csv`. Source lineage remains available under `source/extracted/my-core-legacy/MY Core IG`.

## Normative posture

Questionnaires are normative where a programme requires them. They are not part of generic baseline conformance.
